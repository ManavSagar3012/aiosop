## Decision log

| Decision | Why | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Soft-default tenancy (strict_tenancy=False) | Avoid breaking single-tenant deployments mid-migration | We'll flip to deny-unknown once proven in QA |
| Local env-scan only until HIBP key | No external dependency to land code | Real breach-corpus signal arrives only with provisioning |
