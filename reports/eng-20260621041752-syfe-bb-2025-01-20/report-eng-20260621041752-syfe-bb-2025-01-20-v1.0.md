## Risk Narrative

The Syfe UAT environment presents a moderately complex attack surface with both
client-side information disclosure and authentication flow weaknesses. Four findings
were identified during the reconnaissance phase: two MEDIUM-severity client-side
state exposures (isAdvisor role disclosure and voice call OTP feature control) and
two LOW-severity information leakage issues (rate limit timing disclosure and error
message enumeration). These findings suggest that the authentication layer may not
fully validate client-controlled state on the server side, creating opportunities
for targeted attacks against advisor-tier accounts and potential MFA bypass vectors.
All findings were confirmed through non-destructive observation and pose no immediate
exploitation risk without further authenticated testing.
