**Executive Risk Narrative**

This engagement assessed a single digital asset comprising three endpoints. The assessment identified two distinct security findings, with no critical or high-severity vulnerabilities detected. The overall risk profile remains moderate, as the identified issues, while noteworthy, do not present immediate, exploitable threats to the environment.

The primary findings include a wildcard DNS configuration detected across the assessed infrastructure and a default login vulnerability on the Apache Casbin MCP Gateway. The wildcard DNS configuration may inadvertently expose broader attack surface by resolving to resources outside the intended scope, potentially facilitating subdomain takeover or DNS redirection risks. Additionally, the default login credential on the Gateway service poses an authentication bypass risk if left unmitigated. We recommend implementing DNS zone segmentation, removing wildcard records where unnecessary, and enforcing credential rotation policies on the Gateway service to reduce the attack vector. Ongoing monitoring and periodic reassessment are advised to maintain a robust security posture.
