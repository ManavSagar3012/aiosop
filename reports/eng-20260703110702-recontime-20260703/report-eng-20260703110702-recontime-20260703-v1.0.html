This engagement assessed one (1) digital asset comprising three (3) endpoints. The assessment identified two distinct security findings, with no critical or high-severity vulnerabilities detected. These findings suggest potential misconfigurations in DNS management and authentication controls that, while not immediately exploitable, could introduce risk if left unaddressed.

Ongoing monitoring and periodic reassessment are advised to maintain a robust security posture.
