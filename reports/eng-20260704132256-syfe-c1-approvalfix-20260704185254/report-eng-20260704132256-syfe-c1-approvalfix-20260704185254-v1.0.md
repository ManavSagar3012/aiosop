The assessment identified 58 findings across 1 asset and 3 endpoints. While the majority of findings (57) are informational in nature, the engagement uncovered 1 high-severity vulnerability: Apache Casbin MCP Gateway – Default Login. This finding indicates that the gateway interface is accessible using default administrative credentials, presenting a significant authentication bypass risk that could allow unauthorized actors to gain administrative control over the gateway functionality. The presence of default credentials in production environments is a well-documented attack vector and represents a critical gap in access control hygiene.

Additionally, the scan detected wildcard DNS configurations and WAF presence across the target environment. While these findings are informational, wildcard DNS configurations can facilitate DNS-based attacks such as subdomain enumeration and cache poisoning, and the WAF detection suggests the organization has implemented defensive layers that should be regularly tuned to ensure they effectively mitigate known attack patterns. The informational findings, though not immediately exploitable, provide reconnaissance value to an attacker and should be reviewed to understand the attack surface exposure. Overall, the high-severity default login finding requires immediate remediation to reduce the risk of unauthorized administrative access.
